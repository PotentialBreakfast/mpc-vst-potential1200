# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-15)

First release.

- 12-bit, 26.04 kHz sampling with no anti-alias filter, drop-sample pitch and one shared sample clock.
- PERFORM page: 8 faders (TUNE, DECAY, LEVEL or FILTER for the 8 pads) and 8 pads.
- PROGRAM page: per-pad tune, decay, level, cutoff, filter (DYNAMIC, FIXED, OFF), STRETCH, REVERSE, choke group, START and END trim.
- KIT page: ONE-SHOTS (from a DRUM ONE SHOTS folder, folders.txt, or your Expansions and Samples) with per-pad type, arrows and locks, or a BREAK chopped across the pads with tempo matching, shown as a waveform with numbered chop lines and a moving playhead. NEW KIT, ROLL (MILD, MEDIUM, WILD) and UNLOCK ALL.
- SETUP page: the 45 > 33 trick, CLEAN character, MULTI PITCH on pads 9-16, SAVE KIT (an MPC drum program), RESCAN.
- Four looks: Vintage (new), Print Plate, Night Rack and Colour Block.
- On-screen MANUAL page.
