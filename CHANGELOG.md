# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11)
- New **Hi-Fi** look (the fifth on the LOOK selector, from PotentialBox): walnut cheeks, a brushed charcoal faceplate, raised panels, domed knobs lit in the plugin's colour, moulded cream keys and green glass displays.
- The LOOK selector now has five looks: Print Plate, Night Rack, Colour Block, Vintage and Hi-Fi.

## 1.0.2 (2026-11-15)

- Fixed: the break waveform's chop lines showed the wrong pad numbers (its picture strip was over the skin's size limit).

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-15)

- Every break becomes a 2-bar loop starting on a kick on the 1 (a loop file on its own bar line, a long take on a random strong-beat kick); a 1-bar loop stays 1 bar.
- Loop tempo from the file's length in whole bars when the name has none.
- PERFORM: room between the display and the TUNE / DECAY / LEVEL / FILTER keys.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-15)

First release.

- 12-bit, 26.04 kHz sampling with no anti-alias filter, drop-sample pitch and one shared sample clock.
- PERFORM page: 8 faders (TUNE, DECAY, LEVEL or FILTER for the 8 pads) and 8 pads.
- PROGRAM page: per-pad tune, decay, level, cutoff, filter (DYNAMIC, FIXED, OFF), STRETCH, REVERSE, choke group, START and END trim.
- KIT page: ONE-SHOTS (from a DRUM ONE SHOTS folder, folders.txt, or your Expansions and Samples) with per-pad type, arrows and locks, or a BREAK chopped across the pads with tempo matching, shown as a waveform with numbered chop lines and a moving playhead. NEW KIT, ROLL (MILD, MEDIUM, WILD) and UNLOCK ALL.
- SETUP page: the 45 > 33 trick, CLEAN character, MULTI PITCH on pads 9-16, SAVE KIT (an MPC drum program), RESCAN.
- Four looks: Vintage (new), Print Plate, Night Rack and Colour Block.
- On-screen MANUAL page.
