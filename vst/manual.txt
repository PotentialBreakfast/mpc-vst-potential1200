= START
# A 12-BIT SAMPLER, THE LATE-80S WAY
Every sound is sampled at 26 kHz with no filter in front (the highs fold back as grit) and kept as 12 bits.
Pitch works the old way: a higher pitch plays faster and shorter, a lower one slower and longer.
- Play pads 1-8 (A01-A08). Pads 9-16 play the selected pad at 8 pitches (MULTI PITCH).
- PERFORM: 8 faders for the 8 pads. TUNE, DECAY, LEVEL or FILTER picks what they move.
- KIT: where the sounds come from. ONE-SHOTS from your folders, or a BREAK chopped across the pads.
- Start: open KIT and tap NEW KIT or ROLL. Then play.

= PERFORM
# THE FADERS
Tap TUNE, DECAY, LEVEL or FILTER: the faders jump to that setting of each pad.
- TUNE: -12 to +12 semitones. Pitch changes the length too, as on the old machines.
- DECAY: shortens the tail. FULL lets the whole sound play.
- LEVEL: each pad's volume. All the way down is off.
- FILTER: the cutoff of a DYNAMIC pad. Pads 1-2 are DYNAMIC, 3-6 FIXED and 7-8 OFF to start with.
Tap a pad below the faders to hear it and select it. PROGRAM shows the selected pad.

= KIT
# WHERE THE SOUNDS COME FROM
ONE-SHOTS: a DRUM ONE SHOTS folder on any drive (MPC TOOLS makes one with MAKE DRUM FOLDER), or the folders
listed in Potential1200/folders.txt, else the drum sounds in your Expansions and Samples.
- Each pad has a type (KICK, SNARE, HAT ...). Its arrows step through that type. L locks the pad.
- NEW KIT: new sounds on every unlocked pad. ROLL: MILD, MEDIUM or WILD changes to sounds and settings.
BREAK: 2 bars of a break from your DRUM BREAKS LIBRARY, from a kick on the 1, cut into 8 on the beat or on the hits.
- TEMPO: AS IS, MATCH (TUNE) the old way (faster is higher), or MATCH (STRETCH) to keep the pitch.

= SETUP
# SOUND AND SAVING
- SAMPLE SPEED 45 > 33: the old record trick. The sound is taken fast and slowed back, darker and crunchier.
- CHARACTER CLEAN: no 12-bit or 26 kHz, to hear the difference.
- PADS 9-16: MULTI PITCH plays the selected pad at 0 2 3 5 7 8 10 12 semitones, or OFF.
- SAVE KIT: the 8 pads as you hear them, as an MPC drum program in Potential1200/Kits.
- PROGRAM: STRETCH keeps a pad's length when you tune it. START and END trim it. CHOKE groups cut each other.

= HELP
# IF SOMETHING IS MISSING
- No one-shots: make a drum folder in MPC TOOLS (Plugins tab), then tap RESCAN on SETUP.
- No breaks: a folder called DRUM BREAKS LIBRARY at the top of a drive, then RESCAN.
- A sound that will not load shows CAN'T READ on the status line. WAV, AIFF, FLAC and MP3 work.
- The mix is mono, like the old machines. Each pad plays one sound at a time.
